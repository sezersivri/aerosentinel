About AeroSentinel

1 min · AeroSentinel

What is AeroSentinel?#

AeroSentinel is an automated research intelligence service focused on hypersonic aerodynamics, aerothermodynamics, and reentry vehicle design.

Every 2 days, the system scans top-tier academic databases for new publications, filters them through a strict quality gate, and publishes a curated synthesis of the most important findings.

Sources#

Papers are sourced from:

  • OpenAlex — broad academic coverage
  • Semantic Scholar — citation velocity and influence metrics
  • arXiv — latest preprints from elite institutions
  • NASA NTRS — NASA technical reports and memoranda

Quality Filter#

Only papers meeting strict criteria are included:

  • Published in Tier 1 journals: AIAA Journal, Journal of Fluid Mechanics, Physics of Fluids, Journal of Spacecraft and Rockets, and others
  • From Tier 2 journals only if authored by researchers at NASA, DLR, ONERA, JAXA, or other leading aerospace institutions
  • arXiv preprints only from established research groups

Topics Covered#

Aerodynamic heating, hypersonic boundary layer transition, shock-boundary layer interaction, scramjet aerothermodynamics, reentry vehicle design, catalytic wall effects, ablation and thermal protection, rarefied gas dynamics, and machine learning applications in CFD.

Technical Details#

This site is fully automated, open source, and costs $0/month to operate. Built with Hugo, hosted on GitHub Pages, orchestrated by GitHub Actions, and summarized by Google Gemini.
