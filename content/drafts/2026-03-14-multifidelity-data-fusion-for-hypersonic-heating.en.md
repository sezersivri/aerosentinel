**Type:** 🤖 ML/Heating Prediction | **Relevance:** 88/100

Duan and colleagues introduce an augmented data fusion framework aimed at predicting aerodynamic heating on hypersonic vehicles. This framework combines data enhancement strategies with a multifidelity U-NET (MF-UNet) architecture. The approach addresses the challenges of sparse experimental data and the inherent trade-offs in accuracy and resolution associated with traditional CFD and wind-tunnel measurements. The MF-UNet is developed to optimize multifidelity mapping for high-precision heat-flux reconstruction, while systematic data augmentation expands limited experimental datasets to improve extrapolation capabilities.

The methodology was validated on a hypersonic double-ellipsoid configuration. It successfully integrated three sparse experimental datasets to generate complete surface heating profiles. The framework demonstrated significant improvements over baseline CFD solutions, achieving a surface-averaged normalized root-mean-square error below 3% when compared with experimental measurements. The approach enables full-surface extrapolation, even with limited experimental inputs.

This work provides a practical solution for aerodynamic heating analysis by effectively integrating multifidelity data, particularly sparse experimental measurements, with a deep learning model. The ability to generate complete heating profiles and extrapolate to new conditions from limited experimental data is valuable for design cycles where experimental campaigns are costly and time-consuming.
