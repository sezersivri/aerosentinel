## What is AeroSentinel?

AeroSentinel is an AI-powered research intelligence platform focused on **hypersonic aerodynamics, aerothermodynamics, missile heating prediction, and ML/AI surrogates for CFD**.

Every week, the system scans top-tier academic databases for new publications, filters them through a strict quality gate, and publishes a curated intelligence briefing with structured analysis of the most important findings.

## Sources

Papers are sourced from 7 academic databases:

- **OpenAlex** — broad academic coverage (200M+ works)
- **Crossref** — 70M+ articles, DOI-based metadata
- **CORE** — 200M+ open access articles
- **Semantic Scholar** — citation velocity and influence metrics
- **arXiv** — latest preprints from elite institutions
- **NASA NTRS** — NASA technical reports and memoranda
- **IEEE Xplore** — IEEE conference and journal papers (optional)

## Quality Filter

Only papers meeting strict criteria are included:

- Published in **Tier 1 journals**: AIAA Journal, Journal of Fluid Mechanics, Physics of Fluids, Journal of Spacecraft and Rockets, and others
- From **Tier 2 journals** only if authored by researchers at NASA, DLR, ONERA, JAXA, or other leading aerospace institutions
- **arXiv preprints** only from established research groups
- **Keyword priority scoring** based on thesis relevance: AI/ML in aerothermodynamics (+25), general heating (+15), broader CFD (baseline)

## Analysis Features

Each briefing includes:

- **Relevance scoring** (0-100) based on missile aerothermodynamics and ML/CFD relevance
- **Paper type classification**: Experimental, Numerical/CFD, ML/Surrogate, Review, Analytical
- **Key numbers**: Mach ranges, RMSE percentages, speedup factors, heat flux values
- **Cross-paper connections** and field trend analysis
- **Collapsible methodology details** for in-depth reading
- **Bilingual**: Available in English and Turkish

## Topics Covered

Aerodynamic heating prediction, ML surrogates for CFD, Gaussian process regression, physics-informed neural networks, hypersonic boundary layer transition, shock-boundary layer interaction, scramjet aerothermodynamics, reentry vehicle heating, ablation and thermal protection, conjugate heat transfer, stagnation point heating, and turbulence modeling.

## Technical Details

This site is fully automated, open source, and costs $0/month to operate. Built with Hugo, hosted on GitHub Pages, orchestrated by GitHub Actions, and analyzed by Google Gemini 2.5 Flash.
