## What is AeroSentinel?

AeroSentinel is an AI-powered research intelligence platform built for a specific thesis domain: **"Prediction of Aerodynamic Heating on High-Speed Missiles Using Gaussian Process Based Surrogate Models."**

Every week, the system scans 7 top-tier academic databases for new publications, filters them through a multi-layer quality gate, classifies papers into core and peripheral research, and publishes curated bilingual intelligence briefings with structured analysis -- all driven by a Telegram bot with one-tap approval.

## Thesis Focus

This platform is specifically tuned to track research at the intersection of:

- **Aerodynamic heating** -- prediction, measurement, and simulation of surface heat flux on high-speed vehicles
- **AI/ML surrogates for CFD** -- Gaussian process regression, neural networks, deep learning, and physics-informed methods applied to aerodynamic and aerothermodynamic problems
- **Hypersonic flow physics** -- boundary layer transition, shock-boundary layer interaction, real gas effects, stagnation point heating

Papers directly on these topics get full individual deep-dive reviews (**core papers**). Everything else -- broader CFD, general aerospace, scramjet propulsion, reentry physics -- is synthesized into a flowing academic narrative with in-text citations (**peripheral papers**).

## Sources

Papers are sourced from 7 academic databases:

- **OpenAlex** -- broad academic coverage (200M+ works), with journal tier and institution filtering
- **Crossref** -- 70M+ articles, DOI-based metadata, with thesis/dissertation discovery
- **CORE** -- 200M+ open access articles, including theses and grey literature
- **Semantic Scholar** -- citation velocity, influential citation counts, abstract enrichment
- **arXiv** -- latest preprints, restricted to physics and computational categories with aerospace topic relevance check
- **NASA NTRS** -- NASA technical reports and memoranda (always Tier 1)
- **IEEE Xplore** -- IEEE conference and journal papers (optional, requires API key)

## Quality System

### Journal Tiers
- **Tier 1** (always kept): AIAA Journal, Journal of Fluid Mechanics, Physics of Fluids, Journal of Spacecraft and Rockets, Aerospace Science and Technology, Shock Waves, and others
- **Tier 2** (kept with citation velocity or elite institution): Computers & Fluids, Acta Astronautica, International Journal of Heat and Mass Transfer, Chinese Journal of Aeronautics, and others
- **Tier 0** (blocked, except theses/dissertations): unranked journals

### Scoring
Papers are scored (0-100+) based on journal tier, source, keyword priority match, citation metrics, recency, and abstract availability. Only papers scoring 30+ are included. Papers scoring below 20 are discarded entirely.

### Thesis Discovery
Crossref dissertations and CORE theses bypass the tier-0 filter, enabling discovery of PhD and MSc work directly relevant to the thesis domain. These still pass through scoring, so irrelevant theses are naturally filtered out.

## AI Analysis

Each briefing is generated by **Gemini 2.5 Flash** and includes:

- **Two-tier structure** -- Core papers get full analysis (methodology, key findings, key numbers, limitations, cross-paper connections); peripheral papers are woven into a literature-review-style narrative
- **9 paper type classifications** -- ML/Heating, ML/Aerodynamics, ML/Transition, Numerical/CFD, Experimental, Analytical, Review, Multi-Method, Thesis
- **36 curated tags** -- Strict English-only vocabulary covering research domains, methodologies, physical phenomena, flow regimes, applications, and cross-cutting topics
- **Relevance scoring** (0-100) based on proximity to the thesis domain
- **Critical analysis** -- Limitations field identifies methodological weaknesses and evidence strength
- **Anti-slop filter** -- 24 banned AI filler phrases blocked in prompts and scrubbed from output
- **Bilingual output** -- Every briefing in both English and Turkish

## Telegram Bot

Control everything from Telegram:

| Command | Description |
|---------|-------------|
| `/scout` | Trigger a paper hunt now |
| `/search` | Interactive custom search -- pick from 36 tags, set date range |
| `/bibtex` | Export latest digest as BibTeX citations |
| `/bookmarks` | View your bookmarked digests |
| `/status` | Check latest workflow status |
| `/help` | Show available commands |

Draft previews arrive with one-tap **Publish / Edit / Discard / Bookmark** buttons.
